Proposed AI Feature List – Ellington Properties
1. Sales AI Agent – Phase 1 Priority
1.1 Conversational Salesforce Assistant for Property Consultants
An AI assistant that enables Property Consultants to retrieve Salesforce information through natural-language text queries.
Key features:
Retrieve lead profile summaries, including customer interest, budget, timeline, language preference, source and lead status.
Display previous interactions, notes, appointments, and assigned Property Consultant details.
Provide project information, unit details, payment plans, floor plans, and availability information.
Support text-based queries.
1.2 Smart Inventory Search
Enable Property Consultants to find suitable units quickly based on a customer’s preferences.
Key features:
Search available inventory by project, unit type, budget, size, bedrooms, view, floor, and payment plan.
Display matching inventory with key unit details.
Compare multiple units for the customer.
Identify alternative options when a requested unit is unavailable.
Surface available extra parking bays where applicable.
1.3 Sales Offer Generation
Allow Property Consultants to generate customised sales offers directly through the AI assistant.
Key features:
Generate sales offers as PDF documents or shareable links.
Apply approved discounts and payment-plan adjustments.
Include selected unit details, price, payment schedule, and relevant terms.
Trigger internal approval where discounts or adjustments exceed authorised limits.
Maintain a record of generated offers in Salesforce.
1.4 EOI Creation Assistance
Support Property Consultants in creating Expression of Interest records more efficiently.
Key features:
Capture customer information conversationally.
Pre-fill EOI fields using available lead and customer data.
Support OCR extraction from uploaded customer documents where applicable.
Create customer account records and required verification tasks for Sales Administration.
Flag missing mandatory information before submission.
1.5 Booking Creation and Approval Workflow
Simplify booking creation while retaining required approval controls.
Key features:
Create booking records directly from the AI-assisted conversation.
Support bookings with or without an existing EOI.
Support joint buyer details.
Validate mandatory booking information before submission.
Trigger CEO or authorized approver workflows based on defined business rules.
Notify Sales Administration and relevant stakeholders of booking status changes.
1.6 Voice Agent for Non-Working-Hour Tele sales
A voice AI agent to supplement tele sales operations outside normal working hours.
Key features:
Handle inbound and outbound customer calls outside working hours.
Conduct initial lead qualification using predefined questions.
Capture customer interest, preferred project, budget, timeline, and contact details.
Categorise leads as Hot, Warm, or Cold.
Create or update leads in Salesforce.
Schedule callbacks or meetings for Property Consultants.
Handle frequently asked questions using approved project and sales information.
Escalate complex, negative, or sensitive conversations to a human team member.
Provide call recordings, transcripts, summaries, and lead-disposition reports.
2. Finance and Payment Support – Future Phase
2.1 Proof of Payment Processing Support
Improve the current manual process for allocating bank-transfer payments.
Key features:
Upload and read Proof of Payment screenshots.
Extract payment details through OCR.
Match available information with draft receipts, customer details, and booking records.
Flag uncertain or unmatched payments for Finance review.
Create a review queue for Finance users.
Maintain an audit trail of payment allocation decisions.
2.2 Payment Status Assistant
Provide quick visibility into payment and booking financial status.
Key features:
Retrieve payment status for a booking.
Show outstanding invoice amounts and payment due dates.
Display down-payment, registration-fee, and administration-fee status.
Identify pending payment actions before contract generation.
Alert users when payment information is incomplete or requires Finance validation.
3. Contract and Document Automation – Future Phase
3.1 DocuSign Integration Support
Support document generation and contract processing after payment approval.
Key features:
Retrieve contract and document status from DocuSign.
Trigger generation of URC and SPA documents based on defined workflow conditions.
Notify users when documents are ready for customer signature.
Track pending, completed, rejected, or expired signature requests.
Maintain document links and activity history within Salesforce.
3.2 Customer Document Readiness Check
Validate that required documents are available before progressing to the next sales stage.
Key features:
Check availability of mandatory customer documents.
Identify missing, expired, or incomplete documents.
Notify the responsible Property Consultant or Sales Administration team.
Maintain a checklist of document-completion status.
4. Design and Development AI – Future Phase
4.1 Feasibility and Development Control Regulation Assistant
Reduce risk in plot feasibility studies by centralizing authority-specific development rules.
Key features:
Maintain a searchable knowledge base of Development Control Regulations for relevant UAE authorities.
Identify authority-specific rules for a selected plot.
Highlight plot-specific parameters such as building height, setbacks, parking requirements, and allowable unit mix.
Alert users when regulation information may be outdated or incomplete.
Compare feasibility assumptions against previous projects.
4.2 Portfolio Benchmarking for New Developments
Help the Design team assess proposed project efficiency against Ellington’s existing portfolio.
Key features:
Compare proposed unit mix, efficiency, saleable area, parking ratio, and other key metrics with prior projects.
Identify potential underperformance against benchmark projects.
Generate summary reports for management review.
Highlight areas requiring further feasibility validation.
4.3 AI-Based Drawing Compliance Review
Provide a first-pass review of design drawings against applicable standards.
Key features:
Review drawings against Dubai design codes, authority requirements, and Ellington design standards.
Identify potential non-compliance issues.
Flag missing information, inconsistencies, or deviations from standards.
Generate a structured review summary by drawing, discipline, and severity.
Support large drawing sets and multiple design stages.
4.4 Automated Comment Tracking and Compliance Scoring
Replace the manual process of managing design-review comments.
Key features:
Extract comments from reviewed drawings.
Create a centralised comments tracker.
Assign comments to consultants or internal owners.
Track response, closure status, due dates, and evidence.
Generate a compliance score for each submission.
Trigger resubmission requests when a defined compliance threshold is not met.
Support payment-hold workflow triggers where required.
4.5 Project Launch Data Validation
Reduce mismatches between official project documents and sales-system information during launch.
Key features:
Validate sellable-area schedules before data is uploaded to sales systems.
Compare surveyor data, approved layouts, sales inventory, and marketing materials.
Identify mismatches in unit details, area, pricing inputs, parking, and payment-plan data.
Generate an exception report for correction before project launch.
Maintain a source-of-truth record for approved launch data.
4.6 Parking Inventory Management
Provide visibility and control over extra parking bays used as a sales incentive.
Key features:
Track available extra parking bays by project.
Reserve or allocate parking bays against specific customer bookings.
Prevent duplicate allocation or over-promising.
Provide real-time parking availability to Property Consultants.
Maintain approval and audit history for parking allocations.
4.7 Post-Contract Decision and RFI Knowledge Base
Capture historical project decisions after handover to the site team.
Key features:
Record RFIs, shop-drawing decisions, approvals, and design clarifications.
Create a searchable repository of past decisions.
Link decisions to project, drawing, consultant, and discipline.
Provide contextual recommendations based on similar historical cases.
Preserve decision history for future projects and dispute prevention.
5. Recommended Initial POC Scope
The recommended first Proof of Concept should focus on the Sales AI Agent, with the following initial capabilities:
Salesforce lead-summary retrieval.
Conversational inventory search.
Sales-offer generation.
EOI and booking creation assistance.
Voice agent for lead qualification during non-working hours.
Salesforce activity logging, call transcripts, and reporting.
