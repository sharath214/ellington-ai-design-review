AI Design & Development Opportunities
Process Definition Document
Design process automation & intelligence — feature spec and delivery plan for Ellington Properties
1. Overview
Ellington Properties has mandated Wissda to lead a company-wide AI transformation. This document defines the AI Design & Development feature set: AI-assisted feasibility analysis, RFP and consultant management, design review, contractor tendering, and construction/shop-drawing workflows. The system operates alongside Ellington's existing tools — Salesforce, CAD/BIM platforms, and DocuSign — as an intelligent assistant to Design Managers, automating retrieval, validation, and compliance checking while human approval is preserved at every design checkpoint.
2. Scope & Assumptions
In scope
Stage 1 — Feasibility Study: authority-specific DCR lookup, feasibility template auto-completion, executive go/no-go dashboard.
Stage 2 — RFP & Consultant Appointment: RFP auto-generation, PTC (post-tender clarification) logging and response tracking.
Stages 3–6 — Design Reviews (Concept → Tender Package): AI-assisted first-pass compliance review, design comment tracking, compliance scoring and resubmission triggers, design change impact assessment.
Stage 7 — Project Launch Preparation: sellable-area data sync to Salesforce, marketing-material coordination tracker, target cost-per-sqft publication.
Stage 8 — Contractor Tender & Handover: contractor evaluation scoring, design intent handover package compilation.
Stage 9 — Construction & Shop Drawings: construction drawing validation against approved design, shop drawing review workflow.
Stage 10 — Post-Contract & Parking Inventory: RFI/shop-drawing decision logging, extra parking-bay inventory tracking in Salesforce.
Out of scope
Not: autonomous design approval or rejection — every compliance flag, resubmission trigger, and contractor score is confirmed by a human reviewer before action is taken.
Not: authoring or editing drawings inside CAD/BIM systems — the AI reads, compares, and validates; it does not modify drawing files.
Not: awarding or disqualifying contractors, or negotiating tender terms — evaluation scoring is advisory input to the procurement decision only.
Not: legal or contractual interpretation of RFP, tender, or consultant agreement terms.
Not: changing Salesforce's existing project ownership, assignment, or approval-routing logic.
Assumptions & dependencies
Ellington provides Salesforce sandbox access (API credentials, object/field permissions) at kickoff.
Ellington provides CAD/BIM system access with file retrieval, comparison, and export permissions.
Ellington provides authoritative, version-controlled Dubai Design Code datasets (DM, DDA, DSO) and internal brand-standards documentation.
Historical project data (unit mixes, rental yields, cost benchmarks) is accessible via Salesforce.
DocuSign credentials are provided for contract- and handover-stage workflows.
Design Managers remain the sole approval authority at every checkpoint; the AI is not granted write-approval rights in Salesforce.
